# Lunte

**When you lose it with your AI (more than you usually do), your laptop blows up. Everyone hears it. Own it, or stay a mystery.**

A Mac menu bar app for Claude Code. It's a prototype, with two players so far.

## How it plays
- A stick of dynamite sits in your menu bar.
- Tell your AI off ("I said…", "why did you…") and one piece of the fuse burns.
- Really lose it (a swear at it, CAPS, "!!!") and two pieces burn. If that's the last of your fuse, your laptop goes 💥.
- Your Club gets the Boom on their Macs and throws you a 🧯, 😂 or 🫂. Justify yourself.
- Every Boom makes your next fuse longer, and every calm stretch makes it shorter. Step away for 10 minutes and you get a fresh stick.

## Privacy
Everything runs on your Mac. Lunte never stores or sends your prompts. Only your Booms, reactions and the "why" you write go to your Club. Read [the promise](docs/adr/0001-everything-runs-on-your-computer.md).

## Install
1. Download `Lunte-<version>.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/ProggePal/lunte-app/releases/latest), unzip it, and drag Lunte into Applications. It's signed and notarized, and it updates itself.
2. Open it, click the 🧨 in your menu bar, and click Connect. That adds Lunte's hook to Claude Code.
3. Open a new Claude Code chat and type `this is a test 3 2 1 pow`. That's your first Boom.
4. Join a Club with its six-letter code, or create one, from the house at the bottom of the popover.

You need a Mac with Apple Silicon, and Claude Code.
